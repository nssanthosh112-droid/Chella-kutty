CHELLA KUTTY – PHONE APP (install once, then it lives on your home screen)

Why a website address is needed: phones only allow "Install app", microphone and offline mode for apps
opened from a secure web address (https). A file opened from Downloads cannot be installed.

EASIEST FREE WAY – GitHub Pages (do this once, on the laptop):
1. Make a free account at github.com (if you do not have one).
2. Click "+" (top right) -> "New repository". Name: chella-kutty. Public. Create.
3. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag ALL 6 files from this folder
   (index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, README.txt). Click "Commit changes".
4. Open Settings -> Pages. Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: main, folder / (root). Save.
5. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your app address appears: https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/chella-kutty/

ON YOUR PHONE:
6. Open that address in Chrome.
7. Tap the "📲 Install" button in Chella Kutty (or Chrome menu ⋮ -> "Add to Home screen" / "Install app").
8. Open Chella Kutty from the home screen icon. Allow the microphone when asked.

MOVE YOUR MEMORIES FROM THE LAPTOP:
- Laptop: ⚙ Brain -> "Save my brain (file)". Send the file to the phone (WhatsApp to yourself / Drive).
- Phone: ⚙ Brain -> "Load brain file".

UPDATING LATER: upload the new index.html to the same GitHub repository; the phone picks it up next time it is online.
Note: a public repository means anyone with the address can open the app – but your memories stay only on your phone.
