🌌 Brain-style memory (quantum-inspired)

Understand: every sentence becomes concepts + links (plot —in→ Thanjavur). Grow: each concept gets a 4096-bit code grown from its name – stored nowhere, regrown when needed (a recipe, not a plate). Entangle: links are codes woven together (XOR). Superpose: all links of one concept live in ONE code. 🧬 Offline brain – three-letter language (BitNet)

A real thinking brain that lives inside your laptop, no internet or key needed after one download. Every connection in it is written with only three letters: −1, 0, +1 (Microsoft BitNet b1.58, 2 billion connections) – about 10× smaller than normal AI brains. Needs Chrome/Edge with WebGPU and about 1.5 GB of graphics memory. Download about 1 GB, once; after that it loads from this browser's storage. It is clever in English, weak in Tamil, and much weaker than the online big brain.
